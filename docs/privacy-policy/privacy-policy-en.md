# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to LiloCharge. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the LiloCharge application.

## 2. Data Collection

### 2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following information:

- **Account Data:** Email address, name, phone number
- **Payment Information:** Tokenized payment data (we never store full credit card numbers)
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates (only when using the app)
- **Charging History:** Charging sessions, energy consumption, payments

### 2.2 Technical Information

- Device type and operating system
- Application version
- IP address
- Crash logs

### 2.3 Usage Data

- Map views
- Search history
- App interactions

## 3. Data Usage

We use your data to:

- **Provide Services:** Find charging stations, manage sessions
- **Process Payments:** Secure payments via ArCa, Idram, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Communication:** Notifications about charging completion, new stations, maintenance
- **Improvement:** Enhance app performance and user experience
- **Support:** Answer questions and resolve issues

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

### 4.1 Mapbox

- **Purpose:** Map display and navigation
- **Data:** Location coordinates
- **Policy:** [https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy)

### 4.2 Payment Systems

- **ArCa:** Armenian card payments
- **Idram:** Electronic wallet
- **Apple Pay and Google Pay:** Mobile payments
- **Data:** Tokenized payment information (no full card numbers)

### 4.3 Firebase Cloud Messaging

- **Purpose:** Push notifications
- **Data:** Device token
- **Policy:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Retention Period

- **Account Data:** While account is active
- **Charging History:** 2 years
- **Payment Transactions:** 7 years (tax requirements)
- **Technical Data:** 90 days

### 5.2 Data Security

- We use modern encryption methods
- Data is stored on secure servers in the European Union
- Limited access for authorized personnel only

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** View your personal data
- **Rectification:** Update incorrect or incomplete data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data
- **Export:** Receive a copy of your data
- **Restriction:** Limit certain processing
- **Objection:** Object to certain uses

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lilocharge.am.

## 7. Children's Privacy

LiloCharge is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Location Data

We use your location data only to:

- Display nearby stations
- Provide navigation
- Save charging session location

You can disable location services at any time in your device settings.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking

We use technical cookies to:

- Ensure proper app functionality
- Save your preferences
- Analyze app usage

We DO NOT use advertising cookies.

## 10. Security

We implement the following measures:

- SSL/TLS encryption for all data transfers
- Password hashing with bcrypt (rounds ≥ 12)
- Regular security audits
- Limited server access
- Regular data backups

## 11. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy. In case of significant changes, we will notify you via email or through the app.
